# Project history and contributors

The widget collection was originally developed by Olivier and Jakob for the
CeNTREX experiment. Wang subsequently brought it into the DeMille Lab control
software and developed additional improvements. Shaozhen later extracted the
collection into this standalone Python package, coordinated the licensing
review, and prepared the public release.

This account records the known development history rather than assigning
ownership of individual lines. More detailed names and contribution notes may
be added as historical records are recovered.
