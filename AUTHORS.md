# Project history and contributors

The widget collection was originally developed for the CeNTREX experiment by
[Olivier Grasdijk](https://github.com/ograsdijk) and
[Jakob Kastelic](https://github.com/js216). [Qian Wang](https://github.com/qw372)
subsequently brought it into the SrF control software and developed additional
improvements. [Shaozhen Yang](https://github.com/SHAOZHEN545)
later adapted it for FrAg, extracted the collection into this standalone
Python package, coordinated the licensing review, and prepared the public
release.

This account records the known development history rather than assigning
ownership of individual lines.
