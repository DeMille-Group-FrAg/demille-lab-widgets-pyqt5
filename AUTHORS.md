# Project history and contributors

The widget collection was originally developed for the CeNTREX experiment by
Olivier Grasdijk and Jakob Kastelic. Qian Wang subsequently brought it into the
SrF control software and developed additional improvements. Shaozhen Yang later
adapted it for FrAg, extracted the collection into this standalone Python
package, coordinated the licensing review, and prepared the public release.

This account records the known development history rather than assigning
ownership of individual lines.
